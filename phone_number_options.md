# Phone Number Acquisition Plan: Helferchen

This document outlines the research and recommendations for acquiring a business phone number for Helferchen, optimized for senior trust and operational flexibility.

## 1. Primary Recommendation: Local Landline (Festnetz)
For a neighborhood service targeting seniors, a local landline number is the most trustworthy option.

*   **Provider:** **Sipgate** (Business S or Starter Plan).
*   **Area Code:** 030 (Berlin) - assuming the pilot is in Berlin.
*   **Benefits:**
    *   **Trust:** Seniors associate landline numbers with established local businesses.
    *   **Low Cost:** VoIP landline numbers are very inexpensive (starting at ~5-10€/month).
    *   **Flexibility:** Calls can be forwarded to any mobile phone or handled via a VoIP handset (Gigaset) or app.
    *   **Features:** Includes professional voicemail and call routing.

## 2. Secondary Recommendation: 0800 Number (Toll-Free)
A professional 0800 number can complement the local number for general inquiries.

*   **Provider:** Sipgate or Easybell.
*   **Benefits:**
    *   **Accessibility:** Free for the caller, removing any hesitation regarding call costs.
    *   **Professional Image:** Signals a serious, customer-focused service.
*   **Cons:** Can feel less "personal" or "neighborhood-based."

## 3. Implementation Steps for CEO
As the CMO, I have identified the best path. The CEO needs to finalize the acquisition due to contract/identity verification requirements:

1.  **Select Provider:** We recommend **Sipgate** for its balance of simplicity and professional features.
2.  **Order Number:** Select a local Berlin (030) number.
3.  **Forwarding:** Set up forwarding to the person currently handling intake (manned Mo-Fri 9-17).
4.  **Provide Number:** Once acquired, share the number with the CMO and UXDesigner to finalize the flyers.
