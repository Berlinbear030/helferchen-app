# Helferchen: Physical Flyer Distribution Strategy

This document outlines the strategy for distributing our physical flyers to the senior population. Our goal is to reach them in their daily environments and build trust through local presence.

## 1. Target Audience & Locations
We focus on areas with a high density of senior residents. 
*   **Residential Areas:** Neighborhoods with older housing stock or known senior-living complexes.
*   **High-Traffic Hubs:** Places where seniors spend time or run errands.

## 2. Distribution Channels

### A. Direct Mail (Mailbox Drops)
*   **Strategy:** Targeted drops in specific neighborhoods.
*   **Pros:** Guaranteed delivery into the home.
*   **Cons:** Can be perceived as "junk mail" if not designed well (our high-contrast, professional design helps here).

### B. Strategic Partnerships (Local Points of Interest)
We will request permission to leave stacks of flyers or post them on bulletin boards at:
*   **Pharmacies (Apotheken):** High trust and high frequency of visits.
    *   *Pilot Targets (Steglitz-Zehlendorf):*
        *   **Curtius-Apotheke** (Curtiusstraße 11, 12205 Berlin) - Near S-Bhf Lichterfelde West.
        *   **Zehlendorf Apotheke** (Teltower Damm 19, 14169 Berlin) - Central Zehlendorf hub.
        *   **Stadt-Apotheke** (Teltower Damm 20, 14169 Berlin).
        *   **Apotheke am Asternplatz** (Asternplatz 3, 12203 Berlin).
*   **Doctor's Offices (Hausarztpraxen):** Especially those with a geriatric focus.
*   **Bakeries & Cafes:** Traditional social hubs for the neighborhood.
    *   *Pilot Targets (Steglitz-Zehlendorf):*
        *   **Konditorei Rabien** (Klingsorstraße 13, 12203 Berlin) - Traditional and highly respected.
        *   **Frau Lüske Kaffeehaus** (Baseler Str. 10, 12205 Berlin).
        *   **Café in der Gartenakademie** (Königin-Luise-Straße 49, 14195 Berlin).
*   **Supermarkets:** Community boards near the entrance/exit.
*   **Community Centers & Senior Clubs:** Direct access to active seniors.
    *   *Pilot Targets (Steglitz-Zehlendorf):*
        *   **Bürgertreffpunkt Bahnhof Lichterfelde West** (Hans-Sachs-Str. 4d, 12205 Berlin).
        *   **Gutshaus Lichterfelde** (Hindenburgdamm 28, 12203 Berlin).
        *   **Hertha-Müller-Haus** (Argentinische Allee 89, 14163 Berlin).
        *   **Hans-Rosenthal-Haus** (Bolchener Str. 5, 14167 Berlin).
*   **Churches & Religious Centers:** Bulletin boards and community rooms.

### C. Personal Handouts (High Trust)
*   **Strategy:** Brief appearances at local markets or community events.
*   **Focus:** "We are here to help," not "We are here to sell." A friendly face builds trust immediately.

## 3. Logistics & Execution

### Phase 1: Test Run (Batch of 500 Flyers)
*   **Area:** **Steglitz-Zehlendorf** (specifically the Lichterfelde-West and Zehlendorf-Mitte areas).
    *   **Reasoning:** High density of senior citizens, established residential areas with older housing stock, and strong local community structures.
*   **Methods:** Mailbox drops and 5-10 strategic partnerships.
*   **Goal:** Measure the "Call Rate" to assess initial interest.

### Phase 2: Scale Up (Batch of 2,000+ Flyers)
*   **Area:** Expanded reach across the city/district.
*   **Methods:** Professional distribution services for mailboxes, plus maintained stacks at top-performing partnerships.
*   **Estimated Costs (Berlin Market):**
    *   Shared Distribution: €70 - €90 per 1,000 units.
    *   Exclusive Distribution: €110 - €150 per 1,000 units.
    *   Postaktuell (Deutsche Post): €0.09 - €0.12 per household.
*   **Key Providers:** Flyer-Rocket, HZV, Postservice Socher.

## 4. Phone Number Acquisition (Priority)
To track calls and provide a professional point of contact, we are acquiring a dedicated number.

*   **Primary Recommendation:** Local Berlin Landline (030) via **Sipgate**.
    *   **Reasoning:** Seniors trust local "Festnetz" numbers more than mobile or 0800 numbers for service-based businesses in their neighborhood.
    *   **Status:** **PILOT PHASE:** Using placeholder number `030 / 123 456 78` for all mockups and pilot materials. Real acquisition deferred to Board for live deployment.
*   **Secondary Option:** 0800 Number.
    *   **Reasoning:** Removes cost barrier for the caller, but can feel more "corporate."

## 5. Tracking & Measurement

*   **Coupon Code/Reference:** Ask callers: "Where did you hear about us?"
*   **Inventory Tracking:** Monitor how quickly flyer stacks are depleted at partner locations.
