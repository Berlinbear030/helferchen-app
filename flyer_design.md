# Flyer Design Specification: Helferchen

This document specifies the layout, typography, and content for the physical flyers of Helferchen, optimized for seniors.

## Design Principles (Accessibility Focus)
- **Typography:** Sans-serif fonts for maximum readability (e.g., Arial, Helvetica, or Open Sans). Minimum body font size: 14pt. Header font size: 24pt+.
- **Contrast:** High contrast (e.g., Dark Blue on White, or Black on Light Yellow). Minimum contrast ratio 7:1 for body text.
- **Layout:** Single-column layout for the main message. No cluttered backgrounds or overlapping text.
- **Imagery:** Warm, authentic photos showing a helper and a senior interacting positively. High-resolution images only.
- **Call to Action (CTA):** Primary CTA is a phone number. Should be large, bold, and easy to find.
