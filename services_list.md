# Helferchen: Service Offering Research

This document outlines the proposed services for Helferchen, categorized into technical assistance and everyday household help. These services are specifically designed for seniors and exclude any nursing or medical care.

## 1. Technical Assistance (Tech-Hilfe)
Seniors often feel overwhelmed by modern technology. Helferchen provides patient, jargon-free support to keep them connected and confident.

*   **TV & Entertainment:**
    *   Setting up and explaining the remote control.
    *   Installing and troubleshooting streaming services (Netflix, Mediatheken).
    *   Connecting DVD/Blu-ray players or sound systems.
*   **Smartphone & Tablet:**
    *   General setup and "how-to" sessions.
    *   Installing and using communication apps (WhatsApp, Skype, FaceTime) for family contact.
    *   Managing photos and videos.
    *   Setting up security and privacy features.
*   **Computer & Internet:**
    *   Setting up Wi-Fi and troubleshooting connection issues.
    *   Setting up email accounts and teaching how to use them.
    *   Safe web browsing and scam prevention (recognizing phishing/fake calls).
    *   Online grocery shopping or pharmacy orders.
*   **Smart Home & Gadgets:**
    *   Setting up smart speakers (Alexa, Google Home).
    *   Configuring smart lighting or heating for easier use.
    *   Help with printers, digital cameras, or e-readers.

## 2. Everyday Household Help (Alltagshilfe)
Helferchen assists with tasks that have become physically demanding or time-consuming, allowing seniors to maintain their independence at home.

*   **Cleaning & Maintenance:**
    *   Window cleaning (interior and accessible exterior).
    *   Light housekeeping (dusting, vacuuming, mopping).
    *   Cleaning the kitchen and bathroom.
    *   Changing bed linens and laundry service.
*   **Organization & Safety:**
    *   Decluttering and organizing closets, pantries, or basements.
    *   Home safety checks (identifying trip hazards, checking smoke detectors).
    *   Seasonal decorations (setting up/taking down).
*   **Shopping & Errands:**
    *   Grocery shopping (with or without the senior).
    *   Errands to the post office, pharmacy, or dry cleaners.
    *   Accompaniment to appointments (hairdresser, bank).
*   **Simple Repairs (Handyman):**
    *   Changing lightbulbs and batteries (smoke detectors, remotes).
    *   Assembling small furniture.
    *   Simple fixes (tightening screws, minor oiling).
    *   Plant care (watering, light repotting).
*   **Pet Care:**
    *   Dog walking.
    *   Feeding and cleaning bowls.
    *   Cleaning litter boxes or birdcages.

## 3. Explicit Exclusions (Nicht im Angebot)
To clarify Helferchen's role as a service companion rather than a healthcare provider:

*   **NO Medical Care:** No injections, wound care, or health monitoring.
*   **NO Personal Hygiene:** No assistance with bathing, dressing, or toileting.
*   **NO Medication Administration:** Verbal reminders are allowed, but physical administration is not.
*   **NO Heavy Construction:** No major renovations, roofing, or structural work.
*   **NO Professional Financial Advice:** Help with bank visits is for accompaniment/transport only, not for managing finances.
