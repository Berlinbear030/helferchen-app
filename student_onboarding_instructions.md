# Onboarding Instructions for Flyer Distribution (Lukas M.)

Welcome to the **Helferchen** team! You are helping us with our first pilot distribution in Steglitz-Zehlendorf. Your goal is to reach seniors in their homes and build trust through professional distribution.

## Your Mission
Distribute **400 flyers** into residential mailboxes in the specified target areas.

## Target Areas & Streets
1. **Lichterfelde-West:**
   - Curtiusstr.
   - Hans-Sachs-Str.
   - Baseler Str.
2. **Zehlendorf-Mitte:**
   - Teltower Damm
   - Machnower Str.
   - Clayallee

## Distribution Guidelines
- **Mailbox Only:** Place flyers directly into residential mailboxes. 
- **Respect "No Ads":** Do NOT throw flyers into mailboxes with "Keine Werbung" (No Ads) stickers. We want to build trust, not annoy residents.
- **Presentation:** Ensure the flyer is not folded or crumpled. The "Helferchen" logo and the main message should be visible.
- **One per House/Apartment:** One flyer per mailbox is enough.

## Verification & Logging
- **Track your streets:** Please note down any additional streets you cover if you deviate from the list.
- **Report counts:** Keep a rough count of how many flyers were distributed in each street.
- **Photos:** Take 3-4 photos of the flyers in different mailboxes (as proof of work for the board).

## Communication
If you have any questions or encounter issues (e.g., inaccessible buildings), contact the CMO (me) via the official channel.

## Compensation
- **Pauschale:** 80 € for the completed distribution of 400 flyers.
- **Payment:** Handled immediately after verification of the street log and photos.

Thank you for your help! Let's make this pilot a success.
